# Core Question

Command Center answers:

```text
What changed?
What matters?
What should I do next?
```

Radar answers:

```text
What was discovered?
```

Pipeline answers:

```text
What is progressing?
What requires action?
```

The Intelligence workspace must answer:

```text
What does Atlas understand?
```

This distinction is critical.

If Radar is observation and Pipeline is execution, Intelligence is interpretation.

---

# The Primary Risk

The easiest mistake would be turning Intelligence into:

* Analytics Dashboard
* Reporting Portal
* Business Intelligence Software
* Metrics Warehouse
* Chart Collection

None of those align with Atlas.

Users do not open Intelligence to read charts.

Users open Intelligence because they want meaning.

---

# What Intelligence Owns

Intelligence should own interpretation generated from accumulated observations.

Atlas is responsible for:

```text
Pattern Recognition
Context
Prioritization
Insight
Recommendation
```

not:

```text
Raw Metrics
Reports
Logs
Statistics
```

# Recommended Intelligence Domains

## Opportunity Quality

Questions:

```text
Which opportunities matter most?

Which opportunities are improving?

Which opportunities are weakening?
```

Atlas synthesizes signals.

Not scores.

Not rankings.

Interpretation.

---

## Career Trajectory

Questions:

```text
What direction is my search moving?

What kinds of opportunities are increasing?

What patterns are emerging?
```

This is strategic rather than operational.

---

## Skill Gap Analysis

Questions:

```text
What qualifications repeatedly appear?

What capabilities limit opportunity access?

What would create the greatest opportunity expansion?
```

Atlas identifies patterns across opportunities.

---

## Market Observations

Questions:

```text
What hiring activity is increasing?

What regions are becoming stronger?

What sectors are becoming weaker?
```

Atlas provides context.

Not labor-market reports.

---

## Resume & Document Effectiveness

Questions:

```text
Which resume variants perform best?

Which application approaches generate responses?

What appears to influence progression?
```

Interpretation rather than reporting.

---

## Source Intelligence

Questions:

```text
Which sources produce strong opportunities?

Which sources produce weak opportunities?

Where should attention be focused?
```

Atlas converts collection activity into guidance.

# Proposed Workspace Structure

## Zone 1

Atlas Priorities

Highest-value recommendations.

Examples:

```text
Pursue Structural Design Roles

Expand Search Radius

Strengthen Revit Skills

Follow Up With WSP
```

These are Atlas conclusions.

Not tasks.

---

## Zone 2

Patterns & Observations

Examples:

```text
Structural opportunities continue increasing.

Interview activity strongest in transportation firms.

Bridge-focused roles outperform general civil positions.
```

Atlas explains what it sees.

---

## Zone 3

Career Trajectory

Examples:

```text
Search increasingly aligned with structural engineering.

Compensation trend improving.

Geographic concentration narrowing.
```

Long-term perspective.

---

## Zone 4

Decision History

Examples:

```text
Recommendations accepted.

Recommendations dismissed.

Observed outcomes.
```

Creates feedback for Atlas.

---

## Zone 5

Strategic Context Panel

Selected insight reveals:

* supporting evidence
* related opportunities
* recommendation history
* Ask Atlas entry

# Relationship To Ask Atlas

Intelligence is:

```text
Atlas speaking proactively.
```

Ask Atlas is:

```text
User asking questions.
```

This distinction is important.

Example:

Intelligence says:

```text
Structural opportunities are increasing.
```

User asks:

```text
Why?
```

Ask Atlas answers.

---

# Scalability

The workspace should function with:

```text
20 opportunities
```

and

```text
500 opportunities
```

because intelligence quality should improve as data volume increases.

The user should never feel overwhelmed by data volume.

Atlas should absorb complexity.

# Final Position

Command Center:

```text
What changed?
```

Radar:

```text
What was discovered?
```

Pipeline:

```text
What is progressing?
```

Intelligence:

```text
What does Atlas understand?
```

Ask Atlas:

```text
Explain it to me.
```

This creates the first complete conceptual separation between all major ATLAS workspaces and gives Intelligence a unique role that is consistent with the platform narrative:

```text
ATLAS scans.

Atlas interprets.

Ask Atlas communicates.

Pipeline executes.
```
