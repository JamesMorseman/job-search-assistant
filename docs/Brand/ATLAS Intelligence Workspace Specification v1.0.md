# <a name="purpose"></a>Purpose
The Intelligence Workspace is the home of Atlas.

If:

Radar discovers

Pipeline progresses

Ask Atlas explains

then Intelligence interprets.

The Intelligence Workspace exists to transform information into understanding and understanding into decisions.

It is the primary surface where Atlas becomes visible.

-----
# <a name="core-narrative"></a>Core Narrative
ATLAS scans.

Atlas interprets.

Ask Atlas communicates.

The Intelligence Workspace is the second stage of this sequence.

Its responsibility is interpretation.

-----
# <a name="ownership"></a>Ownership
## <a name="intelligence-owns"></a>Intelligence Owns

-----

# <a name="workspace-definition"></a>Workspace Definition
The Intelligence Workspace is the interpretation layer of ATLAS and the primary home of Atlas.

It owns recommendations, insights, patterns, trends, and decision support.

Its responsibility is not to discover opportunities, manage applications, or conduct conversations.

Its responsibility is to help users understand what matters and determine what should happen next.

The Intelligence Workspace succeeds when users can confidently answer:

What should I do next?

Why does it matter?

What happens if I do nothing?

and leave with a clearer understanding of both their immediate priorities and long-term career trajectory.
